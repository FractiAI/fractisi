Open source

Why we stay open

It might look like a strategy. It is also the stable move—because it is the right thing to do.

The safest path is the open one

We stay open because it is the safest and best thing to do when the systems we are building get more capable. Secrecy can protect a lead for a quarter. It cannot protect a civilization from compounding error. If the work cannot be run, checked, and corrected by people outside the room, the room stays wrong longer than it can afford.

That is not branding. That is risk management for a world where software compounds.

Superintelligence is not a race to exclude

Frontier talk often sounds like competition: close the weights, close the stack, win the moat. We take the opposite bet. Superintelligence worth building is not for beating everyone else. It is for integrating everything—more of the world’s work, more of its verification, more of its correction—into systems that remain accountable.

Integration needs interfaces the outside can touch. Closed stacks integrate only what the owner allows. Open work invites the rest of the map in. That is the posture behind Tiny Footprint Multi-Agent and the research that feeds it.

Open does not mean always free

Staying open means public repositories, published papers, and a Community Workstation anyone can clone to verify the claim. It does not mean every edition is free forever.

Community is free so verification stays free. Developer Pro, Server, and Cloud exist so the people who keep the product alive can keep doing that work. Verify without buying. Buy when the software earns a seat beside your fleet.

Falsifiability is the floor

A skeptic with a laptop should be able to run the same comparison we publish and disagree with us on the same instruments. If only we can reproduce the result, it is not ready to sell.

How to verify

Clone FractiSI-Multi-Agent. Run try (Git + cloud token) → doctor → install → demo → benchmark → report. Read the paper in the library. Join open evaluation if you want partner measurement. Reproducible comparison—without FractiSI operating your box.
