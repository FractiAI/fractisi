Open role

Founding CEO

The agentic cloud is here—and every agent still wants its own full copy of the world. That is when FractiSI Multi-Agent becomes the commercial fight.

The market case

Every serious AI operator is fighting the same shortages: HBM and DRAM constrained, GPU hours expensive and contended, another rack slow and political. Multi-agent systems make it worse: shared policies, maps, and contexts get duplicated per worker.

FractiSI Multi-Agent installs beside the agent runtime the customer already runs. No rip-and-replace. Shared world once. Per-agent difference only. More concurrent agents on infrastructure already paid for. DRAM is the engine; FractiSI Multi-Agent is the product name; Tiny Footprint is the core the market feels.

When capacity is loose, memory-efficiency software is optional. When capacity is tight and agents multiply, it is how you stretch the fleet you cannot expand fast enough.

Why equity is the point

Frontier AI valuations assume infrastructure remains the bottleneck—who runs more agents per watt and per GB, who owns the shared-context layer next to orchestration and inference.

This is an early commercial seat on that layer. Founding equity—often mid-teens to low thirties for a true external CEO at this stage—while the product is real, the research is public, and multi-agent demand is already here. Cash starts modest or deferred until revenue or a raise.

If that trade does not move you, this role will not either.

The seat

Founding CEO—or Co-founder / CEO. Technical founders stay on invention. You build the company: go-to-market, operations, fundraising, first hires, first pilots with agent platforms and inference hosts. See Company.

Who fits
