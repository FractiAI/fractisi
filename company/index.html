Company

A pre-revenue frontier AI lab.

We develop next-generation AI computing architectures and autonomous intelligence systems that use recursive, adaptive computational models to discover patterns, optimize complex processes, and enable more efficient, scalable intelligence across cloud and GPU-accelerated environments.

FractiSI is the next step of FractiAI. There is no product revenue yet. We operate open source, with a freemium model: the Community kit is free, the repositories are public, and paid tiers are proposed, not required.

FractiAI grew as one house with many decks: a sonic ship, a music catalog, builder tools, and an open stack of empirical labs. The public vow on that house is short. Preserve the signal. Refine the pattern. Navigate the unknown. Fair exchange, and a claim only after the pipeline has been run.

FractiSI keeps that record and turns the latest lab into a product a developer can install. That product is Tiny Footprint. This site is the FractiSI operation. The house site is paused, so the supporting papers are hosted here.

What we are building now

A developer kit that sits beside existing AI and asks a practical question: can shared context occupy less memory, and can the working set be ready sooner, without asking the customer to replace their stack? Workstation 0.1 is the free way to test that on your own machine. Server and Cloud are the same question at fleet scale, still in proposed commercial terms.
