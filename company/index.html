Company

Building the shared-world layer for multi-agent AI

FractiSI is pre-revenue. We ship Tiny Footprint Multi-Agent—software that sits next to agent runtimes and serving stacks so many agents share one world instead of copying it.

What FractiSI is

Tiny Footprint Multi-Agent is infrastructure for concurrent agents. The shared organizational context is stored once; per-agent difference stays compact; views are reconstructed on demand. Operators get more agents from the DRAM they already bought—without a rip-and-replace.

We publish open source. Community Workstation is free. Server and Cloud are the commercial path once the product earns a place in production. DRAM is the engine inside the Multi-Agent product—not a second company.

Where we come from

FractiSI spun from FractiAI’s research program—public labs on holographic rhyme, Infinite Octaves, Tiny Gateway, and the multi-agent shared-world receipts that showed large cuts even after gzip. FractiSI exists to take that pattern into market: a product agent platforms can install, measure, and pilot under real scarcity of memory and accelerator hours.

We are not a consulting wrapper. We are not a closed research boutique. We are a product company with a public research spine and a commercial seat still open.

What we are building now

Now: Tiny Footprint Multi-Agent as the flagship—Workstation + empirical multi-agent suite so teams can clone, measure shared-world packs, and refute.

Next: design-partner pilots on agent hosts and Server-class fleets; joint measurement closer to production DRAM; commercial packaging.

Then: optional network fan-out and durable template adapters—only when partners pull. We do not dilute into parallel SQL/networking SKUs up front.
