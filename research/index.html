Method

Public receipts gate product claims.

FractiAI publishes open empirical labs. FractiSI repeats only claims those labs already carry.

FractiSI Multi-Agent is the flagship product surface — Tiny Footprint / Tiny Gateway at Layer A (shared world once, per-agent deltas, selective reconstruct), plus coordination, composition, Git awareness, and approval-gated deploy. Documented. Versioned. Runnable. Not a claim that a catalog constant replaces laboratory physics.

What local labs measured

Laptop GeForce via DirectML: allocation proxy ~81.4% cut; tiny-gpt2 microbench ~1.80× prefill, ~1.37× generate, ~1.17× train step; in-process suite aggregate ~84.5% against an 80% research target. Those sentences stay tied to that hardware.

Workstation companions (v1.4): signature-bucket routing, context-fold hooks, energy proxy, and organizational rhyme-KV (~14.6% matching cells on distilgpt2; claimableShare closed until spliced resume is logit-equivalent). JSON fractions are not KV compression.

HBM fleets, hosted frontier models, and CapEx economics are not in that receipt. They are why partner evaluation exists.
