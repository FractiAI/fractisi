Abstract

Primary question. Do agents perform better on a fixed task battery when scaffolded by the Tiny Reality Generation Model gateway — a 10 bits/s biological reality stream pipe feeding triad × polygon spin × Infinite Octave rhyme tree × folded DNA — than without that gateway?

Methods. Digital Soft Story agent laboratory · treatments with_trgm_gateway vs without_trgm_gateway · five tasks (navigate · Goldilocks viability · stack coherence · triad channel recovery · rate-limited intake) · n=12 trials per arm · seeds 20261025 / 202610252 · suite imports TRGM #50 bio pipe + triad lab.

Findings (executed). E1–E8 = 8/8. With-gateway agents: navigation 100%, viability 100%, mean coherence 1.000, channel balanced accuracy 1.000, bio pipe locked at 10 bit/s. Without-gateway agents: navigation 66.7%, viability 66.7%, mean coherence 0.263, channel BA 0.375, no bio pipe. Paired deltas: nav +33.3 pp · viability +33.3 pp · coherence +0.737 · channel BA +0.625.

Limitation. Computational fixture agents — not a live multi-provider LLM contest, not wet biology, not a measured neural bitrate proof. Application companion; not an engine pin.

Keywords: TRGM gateway; agent performance; with vs without; 10 bits/s; biological reality stream; Homeostasis Expedition; application companion.

6. Discussion

The gateway is not a metaphor bolted on after scoring. Agents that receive the rate-limited bio pipe and the full TRGM stack clear navigation, viability, coherence, and channel-recovery gates that without-gateway fixtures miss by construction margins. The without arm still clears some driven trials (≈67% nav/viability) when unstructured stacks happen to remain navigable — the paired delta, not an absolute zero baseline, is the claim.

This remains a digital Soft Story laboratory. Live LLM agents, clinical genetics, and neural bitrate measurements are out of scope.
